My recommendation

Keep vector, pushed further, with ley light and a mood per region. It keeps every rig and animation we already have, costs almost nothing at runtime, and it's the style I make best. Region 1 stays bright and sunny like Realm of the Mad God. The Hollows turn dusky, the Emberworks glow orange through smoke, and the Stormcrown goes dark, lit by ley lines and lightning, like Hollow Knight. Enemies and their wind-ups stay bright in every region, so fights stay readable.

The "ley-lit" card shows how the dark regions would look. The "pushed further" card shows the daylight ones.

What I can and can't do

  • Everything is drawn as code: shapes, gradients and glows, posed by rigs. That's why vector and cel are my strongest styles.
  • This session has no image-generation model, so I can't paint bitmaps or produce hand-painted textures.
  • For pixel art I place pixels by coordinates and check them in screenshots. It works, but small details and animation frames are where it suffers. Each frame is drawn by hand, so the rigs would be gone.

Free things that would help

  • Screenshots of art you like, from any game or from Pinterest or ArtStation. Send them in chat; I can see images and match their proportions, palette and lighting. This helps most, and it's free.
  • A free AI image app on your phone for mood boards. I'd use what you make as reference only, not shipped as game art, so the game keeps one consistent style and avoids licence questions.
  • Phaser 4's built-in lights and glow filters. They're already in the engine, so the ley-lit look needs no new download.
  • Free CC0 asset packs (Kenney, OpenGameArt): legal and free, but generic, and they won't match the lost civilization. I'd only use them for small things like particle textures, and only with your OK.
  • If you ever want to draw yourself: Inkscape (vector) and Pixelorama (pixel) are free.
