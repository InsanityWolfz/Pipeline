The wanderer and the tube

Style C, clean vector. The player is a hooded wanderer of the lost civilization: a rust, gold-trimmed cloak over a pale robe, wrapped forearms and heavy boots. There is no face, only the same ley glow as the tube, burning brighter with each shot. The cloak trails and flaps harder while firing, and the brass reliquary tank on their back feeds the tube through the ringed hose.

Cloak:
Point at the scene to aim, and press to fire. Left alone, it fires on its own.

Questions for this round

  • Open: is the lean and shove per shot enough, too much, or should walking slow while firing?
