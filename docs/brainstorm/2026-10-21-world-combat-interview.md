# World and combat interview (053–054)

Oct 21, 2026 · Shaun's answers to the spec interview on themes D and E of docs/brainstorm/2026-10-21-playtest-notes.md. Recommended options are marked (rec).

## Round 1: world and bosses (053)
- **Map size:** the grass region becomes a circle about twice today's overworld area, the Warden in one quadrant, pylons and landmarks spread round it; the Hollows a ring around it of similar width (rec).
- **Boss doors:** each arena fully walled with a big door. Before its unlock (the shrine claim for the Warden, the Hollows barrier for the Bulwark) the door shows sealed, with a hint of what opens it; after, E at the door enters, and inside it locks like the Bulwark's (E to leave, the fight resets) (rec).
- **Paths and ley lines:** one connected road network without crossings or doubling up, joining the foundry, landmarks and arenas; ley lines as smooth single curves from each pylon home, never overlapping a path or each other (rec).
- **Rimestone pylon:** the Frozen Grotto gets a pylon to claim, guarded like other sites, supplying rimestone to a foundry port (rec).

## Round 2: combat readability (054)
- **Star shot:** on a hit, 5 small ley shards fly out evenly spaced, visible in flight, skipping the enemy that was hit, each dealing half the bolt's damage to the first other enemy it touches, out to about 3 tiles (rec).
- **Homing bolts:** about 60% of a plain bolt's speed, same turn rate (rec).
- **Pool arrow:** an arrow through a bomb pool catches fire: a burning tip and a short ember trail for the rest of its flight, a small flash on hit (rec).
- **Immune health bar:** blue with a shield icon only while nothing can damage the enemy (a burrowed Burrower, an add still rising); not for shielded fronts (rec).
