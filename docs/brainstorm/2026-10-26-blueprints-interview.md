# Blueprints interview

Oct 26, 2026 · Shaun's answers (AskUserQuestion), for spec 063.

Why now (Claude's recommendation, Shaun agreed): design.md promises fast retooling ("Layouts can be saved and rebuilt quickly, like a twin-core breaker for a boss. Rearranging a line is fast, so retooling for a fight never takes long"). The bosses are layout puzzles, and 062's material tiers make full refits common.

1. **What a blueprint saves:** a dragged area. In build mode you drag a box over part of the foundry and save the pieces inside, so blueprints can be mixed (a Bulwark bomb line plus a normal arrow line).
2. **Placing:** all at once. A ghost follows the cursor, and a click places every piece instantly (pieces are free). Tiles already taken are skipped, shown in red first.
3. **Unlock:** restore a Blueprint table, a new research diagram on the foundry wall.
4. **Slots:** 8, auto-named after what the line makes ("Frost arrows") with its round's icon. No typing; delete or overwrite from the list.
5. **Transform:** R rotates the ghost 90°, F mirrors it left-right.
6. **Area pick-up, in the same spec:** drag a box to pick up every piece inside at once; their contents go to the stash. Saving and clearing a line are the same drag, with a choice of Save, Pick up, or both.
7. **The table's cost:** 4 ancient gears and 2 aqueduct valves.
