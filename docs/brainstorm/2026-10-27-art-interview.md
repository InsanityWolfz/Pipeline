# Art pass interview

Oct 27, 2026 · Shaun's answers (AskUserQuestion), for specs 074–081. The style sample page he chose from is https://claude.ai/artifact/FLFrch88phbsfMvy8h65ou, saved as docs/art/style-test-2.html.

## Look

1. **Style:** Shaun agreed with the recommendation: keep clean vector art, pushed further, with ley light and a mood per region.
   - Region 1 stays sunny and bright, like Realm of the Mad God.
   - Stonefall Hollows is dusky.
   - The Emberworks glow orange through smoke.
   - The Stormcrown is dark, lit by ley lines and lightning, in the Hollow Knight direction.
   - Enemies and their wind-ups stay bright and readable in every region.
   - He said Realm of the Mad God's low resolution is too small for how much is on screen, and that Hollow Knight "doesn't sound bad".
2. **Region moods:** as proposed (the four moods above).
3. **Outlines:** keep the thin ink outline.
4. **Dynamic light:** yes, with a quality setting. Ley lines, fires, the tube's shots, the wanderer's face glow and bosses' attacks light the ground and enemies near them, and a setting turns it down on slow laptops.
5. **Shading:** soft gradients plus a rim light, as in the "pushed further" sample.

## The wanderer

6. **Redesign:** the wanderer becomes an "arcane ranger": agile and light-armoured, a hooded scout with ley-etched gear.
   - It keeps the faceless cyan glow in the hood.
   - It keeps the minigun tube, the hose and the back tank.
   - Colours: "let you propose". The spec offers two or three colour schemes to choose from.
7. **Armour on the body:** rarity trim only. The cloak's trim and glow change with the rarity of the best armour worn.
8. **Character size:** keep it, about 42 px tall in a 640×360 view.

## Enemies and animation

9. **Enemies and bosses:** polish all of them, with a family look per region (shared materials and colours).
10. **Player animation:** all four areas: the walk and run cycle; firing (recoil, barrel spin, muzzle flash, hose pulse); the roll, hits and KO; and idle life.
11. **Animation feel:** snappy and exaggerated, with squash and stretch and anticipation.
12. **Enemy animation:** clearer wind-ups, hit reactions and deaths. Idle life was not picked.
13. **Deaths:** enemies dissolve into ley motes in their region's colour.
14. **Hit feedback:** "fine how it is for now", so it doesn't change.

## Effects

15. **Particles:** moderate: impacts, deaths, muzzle flashes, machine sparks and ambient motes.
16. **Ammo:** every ammo looks different, with its own trail and impact, including frost, homing and the material tiers.
17. **Screen shake:** none, except that "the only time a screen shake makes sense is like during a boss spawn animation".

## World

18. **World detail:** moderate. Paths and fight space stay clear.
19. **Ground:** blend and vary it: soft edges between terrains, worn paths, patches and cracks.
20. **Ambient life:** swaying grass and trees, moving water and ley, and weather per region: dust in the Hollows, ash and embers in the Emberworks, rain and lightning in the Stormcrown. No critters.
21. **Landmarks:** a few big set pieces per region.

## The foundry, bosses and HUD

22. **Foundry:** "the machines and conduits look pretty good as they are, maybe just a little extra to match the moodier and pushed vector style". Add heat, steam and glow, and ley pulses along the flow. The mood is a warm workshop lit by torches and forges.
23. **Boss intros:** a spawn animation (the one place screen shake is used) and a name card, about 2–3 s.
24. **Boss phases:** a visible change: a colour shift, a glow, a new pose or new parts.
25. **HUD:** polish the stone tablets.

## Order, performance and review

26. **Order:** "look first, then life", as eight specs, each played and merged on its own (the roadmap below).
27. **Performance:** it must run on a modest laptop, with a quality setting.
28. **Review:** "Just build it". No sample pages before each spec; Shaun reviews each one in the playtest.

## Roadmap: eight specs

1. **074, light and moods:** lighting, the region moods and ground blending, plus the Graphics quality setting.
2. **075, the wanderer:** the arcane ranger redesign, its colour schemes, rarity trim and the four animation areas.
3. **076, enemies and bosses:** a family look per region, clearer wind-ups, hit reactions and ley-mote deaths.
4. **077, the world:** props, landmarks as set pieces, ambient life and weather.
5. **078, ammo and particles:** a trail and impact for every ammo, and the moderate particle set.
6. **079, the foundry:** the warm torch- and forge-lit mood, heat, steam, glow and ley flow pulses.
7. **080, boss intros and phases:** the spawn animation with its screen shake and name card, and visible phase changes.
8. **081, the HUD:** the stone tablets polished.
