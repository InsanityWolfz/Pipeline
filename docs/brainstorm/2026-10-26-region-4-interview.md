# Region 4 interview: the Stormcrown

Oct 26, 2026 · Shaun's answers (AskUserQuestion), for specs 064–066. Shaun: "let's get the region 4 spec done so you have plenty to do."

1. **Theme:** the Stormcrown. Rod-studded heights where the ley lines surface as weather, with the Lightning rune and the Conductor, the design doc's "evasive movement" boss, answered by homing ammo.
2. **Third material tier:** stormsteel. Ley-steel tempered with the region's stormglass in a new machine (a Tempering coil) makes stormsteel, and ley glass charged the same way makes storm glass. The same machines are used downstream, at ×1.6 over steel.
3. **Split:** three specs, like 060–062. 064 is the region, stormglass, the Lightning rune, the Conductor and the 5th entrance; 065 is its four enemies; 066 is stormsteel.
4. **Danger:** Shaun: "Make this place a step harder even so it really challenges players im thinking like x10". Ring 5 is ×10 enemy health and damage (the Emberworks is ×4).
5. **Gate:** the Forgewright's first kill drops the Stormcrown's ramp barriers.
6. **Lightning:** chain 2 at half. Each Lightning hit arcs to up to 2 more enemies, each within 3 tiles of the last, for 50% of the hit, never the same enemy twice. It is paid off by two Ley-lane talents: "+1 chain" and "+1 tile chain range".
7. **The Conductor, as proposed:**
   - A robed storm construct with a rod staff, in the Crown Spire. 6,000 health.
   - Six lightning rods ring the arena. Every ~4 s it blinks to another rod, with a flash telegraph at the target rod 0.75 s before.
   - Rods have 200 health and regrow in 20 s. A broken rod can't be blinked to; with 2 rods left it only hops between them.
   - Attacks: an arc lash (a telegraphed line), a storm call (red circles on the player, striking after 1 s), and a rod chain (lightning running rod to rod).
   - Enraged at half health, it blinks every ~2.5 s.
   - Drops: the Conductor core (the 5th entrance), stormglass and the region's part. Altar refight for 8 stormglass.
8. **Places, as proposed:**
   - The Stormcrown is ring 5, the highest: wind-scoured rock, iron rods, a slate-violet glow with cyan ley.
   - Stormglass is harvested from a rod only in the seconds after lightning strikes it.
   - The Rod Field (resource site); the Weathervane (claimable, supplies stormglass); the Broken Sky-bridge (a ruined builders' bridge over a chasm); a Ley Spring; the Crown Spire (the Conductor's arena).
   - Machine part: the storm coil. The world grows again (about 290 tiles), and the intake grows to five entrances.
9. **065's enemies, as proposed:**
   - Thunderhawk: flies over walls and dives down a telegraphed line.
   - Static mites: come in threes and zap on contact; a killed mite discharges into its neighbours.
   - Storm sentinel: a slow rod-turret calling strike circles from range.
   - Gale ram: a front cone gust that pushes back and slows.
   - Packs: 12 of 4–6, at most 1 sentinel and 1 ram.
   - Drops: always 1 stormglass, 20% cinderite, 4% storm coil, the schematic roll and 10% gear.
   - The Weathervane gets a guard pack; champions in 1 in 3 packs.
10. **066's stormsteel, as proposed:**
    - Tempering coil: 1 ley-steel + 1 stormglass → 1 stormsteel; 1 ley glass + 1 stormglass → 1 storm glass.
    - The same machines downstream, by input, in the same counts.
    - ×1.6 over steel (×2.56 over stone), with the same behaviour, stages and talents.
    - Unlocked by tempering once by hand.
    - Three goals after the Conductor.

Claude's note at the interview: at ×10, the Stormcrown is 2.5× tougher than the Emberworks while stormsteel adds 1.6× over steel. Fights there run about 1.6× longer even fully equipped, by design.

## After the playtest build (2026-10-26)

11. Ring 5's damage: Shaun: "yes lower to x7 or x8 I want the difficulty to start to be increased more per area not just a linear increase every region". He picked ×8 damage, with health staying ×10.
12. Scaling rule: each region steps up by more than the region before. Written into design.md ("Difficulty by distance") with his OK. Today's steps: health ×1.56 (the Hollows), ×1.6 (the Emberworks), ×2.5 (the Stormcrown); damage the same, then ×2.
