# Region 3 brainstorm: the Emberworks

Oct 22, 2026 · Claude, from Shaun's spoken notes. Ideas only: nothing here is design until Shaun approves it.

## Shaun's direction (summarised from voice)

- Today a round has three steps: the family (arrow, bomb, ley bolt, landmine), the volley (how many entrances), and one stage (frost, cluster, and so on).
- Talents already give combo behaviour, but they don't ask anything of the foundry. New vertical power should come from the factory.
- The idea: **material tiers.** Harder regions give better raw materials. You bring them home, process them in a new machine, and feed the result into the lines you already have. Stone arrows become steel arrows: same behaviour, more base damage.
- Why it's good: you keep your build. A frost arrow and bomb player upgrades the base and keeps the stages, the spread and the talents.
- The gradual switch is the fun part: one steel lane in the middle while stone still feeds the sides, then a full refit once you've explored enough.
- The worry: sorting one furnace's output across several lines might only appeal to Factorio fans.
- Later tiers add one more processing step each (steel, then something strange like void).

## The region

**The Emberworks.** Up the cliffs past the Hollows: the builders' smelting terraces, where ley still feeds the old forges. Ash fields, slag rivers, chimneys and terraced kilns. It's the third ring round the foundry, behind a barrier the Bulwark's core opens.

Why this theme: it is where the builders made their metal, so it's the natural place to learn steel.

## The vertical: ley-steel

- **The recipe:** ley-iron (from the Hollows) plus cinderite (from the Emberworks) makes ley-steel in a new **Smelter**. Needing both keeps the Hollows' pylons worth having, and pulls the player across regions as the design doc's "Regional pull" asks.
- **Manual first:** smelt one ley-steel by hand at the bench to unlock the Smelter, as every machine unlocks today.
- **One new machine, at the front of the line.** Steel goes into the parts makers you already have:
  - steel arrowhead → steel arrows
  - steel casing → steel bombs
  - steel plate → steel landmines
- **Ley bolts need their own answer** (they have no stone or metal part). Options: a kiln fuses ley dust into ley glass for glass bolts; or bolts stay the ley family's "no metal" identity and get their tier from a later region.
- **Same behaviour, more damage.** A proposal: ×1.6 base damage per tier, so the tier keeps pace with the region's enemy multiplier.
- **Rounds read their tier:** "Steel frost arrow", with a new look, a heavier sound and a bigger hit number, so the upgrade is felt at once.
- **Mixing works:** each entrance has its own reservoir, so one steel lane among stone lanes just works.

### Keeping it fun for players who aren't Factorio fans

- Each line can have its own Smelter. Splitting one Smelter's steel across lines is optional depth, never required.
- The Smelter is the only new link. Everything after it stays as built.
- Steel starts scarce (drops and hand-gathered cinderite), and a claimed pylon makes it plentiful. The one-steel-lane stage happens by itself.

## The horizontal: the Fire rune

Cinderite also makes the **Fire rune**, the reserved Fire keyword (burns over time and spreads). The design doc already says a fire rune brings fire back to bombs.

One material, two uses: cinderite for steel or for fire. It's a real choice early, until the region is claimed.

## The foundry hook

- **The Bulwark's wing becomes the forge room.** It's open but empty (034 left its contents for later). The Smelter, and anything else hot, lives there.
- **Optional: heat.** A brazier burns cinderite, and machines touching it craft faster. Layout becomes packing around the fire. Keep this for a later spec if the Smelter is enough.

## Enemies (four, like the Hollows)

- **Cinder mote:** drifts toward you and bursts into a fire pool on death; lure it into a pack.
- **Bellows hound:** dashes in a line, leaving a burning trail.
- **Slag golem:** slow and tough; splits into two small golems on death. Burst and Cluster clean them up.
- **Kiln-tender:** repairs nearby constructs; kill it first (homing helps).

## Landmarks

- **Cinder vents:** cinderite you can only gather while a vent is open; a vent flares on a timer, with a telegraph.
- **Slag river:** a crossing with few bridges; good for pack fights.
- **A ruined outpost forge:** a miniature foundry that tells some of the builders' story; a Ruined Cache sits inside.
- A Ley Spring and the region's claimable pylons, as before.

## Drops

- **Machine part:** a crucible shard, or a forge rivet (to name).
- **Schematics:** Reservoir III, Smelter speed.

## The boss: the Forgewright

A huge smith construct at an anvil, with a bellows on each side of its back.

- **Heat gauge:** it climbs as it fights. At full heat, its hammer floods the arena with fire pools.
- **The puzzle (twin cores):** hit both bellows within half a second to vent its heat and open its core for a damage window. A three-lane fan at the right range hits both at once. This is the twin-core boss the design doc names first.
- **Raw damage still works:** a bellows broken alone patches itself after a few seconds, so a strong enough single line can break both in time, more slowly.
- **Cold helps:** Cold hits slow the heat gauge. That would be Cold's first content payoff, which the keyword list is still missing.
- **Its core opens the 4th intake entrance**, as the design doc already says.

## Further tiers (later regions)

Each tier adds one processing step, never more:

- stone → ley-steel (Smelter)
- ley-steel → something stranger, for example void-steel (Smelter, then a void well)

## Open questions for Shaun

1. The theme: the Emberworks (forges), or another?
2. Steel from ley-iron plus cinderite (the Hollows stay useful), or a new ore found only in the Emberworks?
3. Ley bolts: glass bolts from a kiln, or no tier until a later region?
4. Per-tier damage: ×1.6, or another number?
5. Heat adjacency now, or later?
6. The boss: the Forgewright as written, or another of the earlier list's ideas?

## Shaun's answers (2026-10-22)

1. The Emberworks: yes.
2. Ley-steel from ley-iron plus cinderite: yes.
3. Ley bolts get an upgrade: glass bolts from a kiln.
4. ×1.6 per tier, to tune later.
5. No brazier.
6. The Forgewright is the boss.
7. Open: what "the wing" means. Today the Bulwark's "sealed wing" is only a door drawn on the foundry's north wall; its first kill marks it open, but there is no room behind it. Options put to Shaun: the Smelter and kiln are ordinary build-bar pieces and the wing waits; or the Bulwark's door opens onto real new floor where they live.
