# Guided-tutorial interview (058)

Oct 22, 2026 · Shaun's answers to the spec interview on theme C of docs/brainstorm/2026-10-22-playtest-notes.md. Recommended options are marked (rec); Shaun's own words are quoted.

## Before the interview
- **Build lock:** "building is locked until after you make the frost arrows portion on the tutorial." The guided ley bolt and frost rune steps are the only exceptions.
- **Grouping:** two fixes (the New game crash, the diagonal flicker), 058 the guided tutorial (C), then a world-polish spec (D). "every thing else is good lets begin the interview".

## Round 1
- **New entrance:** the Warden's core restores the left entrance only, during the tutorial (rec).
- **Ghosts:** strict: only the highlighted piece, only on its ghost tile, facing the right way; anything else is refused with a short hint (rec).
- **Talent step:** cuts in as the current goal as soon as the first point arrives; once one is spent, the chain picks up where it was (rec).
- **Later goals kept:** craft frost arrows by hand (before the frost rune), find the Husk Hive and claim it, and the Hollows run to the end (reach Stonefall Hollows, the Cistern Terrace, the Bulwark, the third entrance, feed all three). Dropped: craft homing or split ley bolts; "Claim a second pylon" and "Run a supply port into your line" (the Grotto's pylon and the ley bolt step cover them).

## Round 2
- **After the Warden:** claim the shrine's pylon, fast-travel to the foundry from it, refill the hoppers in the foundry (only if the example line has run dry; otherwise skipped), restore the left entrance, put ley bolts into it (rec).
- **End state:** when all three entrances are fed, the goal panel and the waypoint arrows go for good; the minimap stays (rec).
- **Unlock banner:** crafting recipes only (anything new in the C menu); several at once share one banner (rec).
- **Old saves:** a save that has already restored the second entrance builds freely and keeps its goals; earlier saves get the new guided steps (rec).
