## What felt good
- The title screen and Settings look good; the build-mode controls and the Splitter work.
- Farming the many Husks at the Rime spring for resin with bombs, arrows and a talent "felt pretty good".
- Beating the enraged Bulwark with only arrows after running out of bombs: tough, and satisfying.

## Claude's reading, by theme

**A. Bugs and quick fixes**
1. Character art: arms differ in size facing forward, shoulders bleed into the head, the belt doesn't wrap round (reads as a stick).
2. Hover name tags sit too high and cover the E prompt; make them smaller and place them clear of controls.
3. Discovery banners cover the player; move them to the top of the screen.
4. F11 fullscreen flickers scroll bars on and off.
5. The end-of-playtest card hides the cursor (it draws behind the card).
6. Gear tooltip stays when the mouse moves onto it; it should go when the mouse leaves the gear square.
7. Holding Shift over gear shows no breakdown (040's Shift breakdown).
8. E should also close the inventory and hopper or crate screens (as Esc does).
9. E on a hopper or crate should open the one under the cursor, within about 3 tiles, not the nearest to the player.
10. Anything picked up, taken from a crate, or otherwise gained inside the foundry should count as safe (banked) at once.
11. The second-line goal didn't count when fed; reword it to "feed ammo into the new entrance", and point its arrow at the entrance just restored (left or right), not the middle.
12. Crates show 8 slots but cap at 100 items; make all 8 slots usable.
13. Gear inventory pages should scroll instead.
14. Inventory items need a hover tooltip with their name.
15. Mending (talent): no sparkle while regenerating.
16. The ward (gear) ring shakes as the player walks; keep it still, with a small shield icon while ready; the expand-on-hit is fine.

**B. Guidance (where to go, what to do)**
1. Edge-of-screen waypoint arrows to the current objective, pointing at the foundry door when the target is on the other side of it.
2. An arrow over the target in place (the bench, a hopper), with resource icons on the hoppers for the refill goal.
3. Objective sub-steps: "Craft bombs and ley bolts" comes before entering the foundry, with "Collect stone" or "Collect ley crystal" sub-notes when the player lacks them.
4. Harvestable trees and rocks need a glow or a distinct look.
5. Minimap: fog of war (revealed once seen, for good); no water pools; discovered pylons and arenas shown; hover labels on minimap markers.
6. A foundry minimap where the overworld one would be: belts as lines, machines as dots, plus the tube, bench, stash and entrances.
7. Right-click teaching: replace the banner with a small mouse icon (button highlighted) beside the carried bar, and on first foundry entry animate the old left-click arrow slot shrinking into the lower bar while the intake's slot takes its place.

**C. Foundry and logistics**
1. Underground belt: an entrance and exit up to 5–6 tiles apart in a straight line, with the tunnel shown while placing and on hover (Factorio). Shaun found mixed lines impossible without crossing belts.
2. Remove the sorter (never used). The merger probably goes too ("we could keep it for now… I think the merger goes").
3. The stash as a two-panel inventory screen for long-term storage.
4. Move the identification bench next to the door; redraw it as an armor rack or workbench with a magnifying glass.

**D. World and bosses**
1. The map becomes round: the grass region a circle with its boss in one quadrant and pylons scattered; the stone region (Hollows) the ring around it, like a donut, with its arena in the ring. Much bigger, with room to explore.
2. The foundry's entrance should look like a big, obvious place to go in.
3. Ley lines and paths: no overlapping, crossing lines; one clean path.
4. Ley lines carry little icons of the resources the pylon supplies instead of the circle motes.
5. Boss doors: the Warden's arena fully walled (no gaps either side) with a door; E at the door enters; inside, the arena locks as the Bulwark's does (E at the door leaves). The Bulwark gets the same door.
6. A rimestone pylon near the Rime spring.

**E. Combat readability and ammo**
1. An enemy that can't be damaged (a burrowed Burrower) shows a blue health bar with a shield icon; Shardbacks don't (they can be hit from behind).
2. The Bulwark's back wave: shade its area red.
3. The Pool-arrow talent needs a visual: arrows through a bomb pool look buffed (a little fire).
4. Homing ley bolts fly slower so they have time to home.
5. Star shot: on a hit, five visible ley shards fly out in five directions and hit enemies around the target (not the one hit), for about half damage.

**F. Title screen**
1. A little more movement on the wanderer; a bigger Pipeline logo and bigger buttons.
