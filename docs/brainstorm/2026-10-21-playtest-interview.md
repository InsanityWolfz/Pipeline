# Live-playtest interview (050–052)

Oct 21, 2026 · Shaun's answers to the spec interview on docs/brainstorm/2026-10-21-playtest-notes.md. Recommended options are marked (rec); Shaun's own words are quoted.

## Round 1
- **First batch:** 050 quick fixes and title screen (themes A and F), 051 guidance (B), 052 foundry logistics (C) (rec). World reshape and boss doors (D) and combat readability (E) come next.
- **Underground belt reach:** up to 5 tiles between entrance and exit, in a straight line; the tunnel shows while placing and on hover (rec).
- **Sorter and merger:** remove both, and the merger's research (rec).
- **Waypoint arrows:** always, for the current goal's target: an edge-of-screen arrow while it's off screen (via the foundry door when it's on the other side), an arrow over it when on screen (rec).

## Round 2
- **Harvestable nodes:** a soft pale outline glow and an occasional sparkle on harvestable nodes only; a used-up node loses it until it regrows (rec).
- **Minimap fog:** any tile that has been on screen is revealed for good; pylons, the foundry and arenas show as icons once discovered; water and other scenery stay off the minimap (rec).
- **Sub-steps:** "do the indented sub lines and maybe even include a little picture of the resource they have to get so its very clear."
- **Stash:** a big grid like the inventory, 8 wide, scrolling, above the inventory, same clicks both ways (rec).
