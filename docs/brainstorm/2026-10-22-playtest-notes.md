## Claude's reading, by proposed PR

**A. Fix: the title screen's New game after Quit to title (fix track, first)**
1. Quit to title, then New game: a black screen with a blank button. Reproduced by Shaun twice.

**B. Fix: the walking sprite flickers on diagonals (fix track)**
1. Walking one diagonal with the cursor on another, the sprite flips back and forth every frame.

**C. Spec: the guided tutorial ("almost brain dead")**
Wording
1. "Fill the tube from your line" becomes something like "Wait for the tube to fill with arrows" (the example line fills it unaided).
2. "Refill the hoppers with wood, stone and fiber" says the hoppers are in the foundry.
3. "Head back out — the foundry keeps running" becomes "Leave the foundry" (no dash); the next goal shows as soon as the player is out, no wait.
4. "Breach Stonefall Hollows" becomes "Reach Stonefall Hollows".
5. The pylon's "Send haul home" becomes "Send inventory to stash".

Order and new steps
6. After identifying the armor: equip one piece at the bench, with arrows in its screen on what to click.
7. After the Warden: claim the shrine's pylon first, then refill the hoppers (today it goes straight to the refill).
8. After the first pylon (or the Warden): fast-travel to the foundry from a pylon, so players learn the waypoints.
9. A talent step when the first point comes in: "Press T to spend a talent point"; done after one is spent. The first point is level 2 (40 XP), which lands somewhere in the ruin field or the shrine; the build pins it down with the tutorial bot.
10. The second-entrance steps merge into one named step, "Put ley bolts into the second entrance" (no "Feed ammo into the new entrance", "Feed two entrances" or "second kind of ammo" steps): ghost pieces show the whole line, from the ley crystal port through its machines into the new entrance, with arrows on the build bar for what to pick.
11. Rimestone: go to the Frozen Grotto, gather rimestone, claim the Grotto's rimestone pylon.
12. Frost rune into the arrow line, step by step: an arrow on the belt leaving the arrow assembler with "Right click to remove"; an arrow on the frost rune in the build bar; a ghost spot for it; then ghost belts from the rimestone port, with a ghost underground belt crossing the ley crystal line from step 10 (the player's first underground belt).
13. Build lock: in the foundry, building stays locked from the first visit until the frost arrows run (step 12). The only exceptions are the guided steps 10 and 12, and during them only the pieces those steps need can be picked. After the frost arrows run, building opens fully (Shaun, 2026-10-22: "building is locked until after you make the frost arrows portion").
14. The goals end when all three entrances are fed: no frontier line ("Explore beyond the shrine") and no goal panel after that.

Placement
15. The waypoint arrow over a target overlaps its E prompt; move it clear.
16. The foundry's goal panel still sits low: put it right under the minimap's space.
17. A new crafting recipe shows a banner at the top, where the area banners go: "Unlocked cluster bomb", "Unlocked frost bomb"; several at once is fine.

Open for the interview
- Step 10's ghost: the ley bolt line's exact pieces (Shaun: "a thing for dust, and then a shooter"; the data's real chain decides), and where the ghosts go so they don't fight the example arrow line.
- Step 8's place: after the shrine's pylon (Shaun: "after getting the first pylon to the first boss").
- What happens to today's later goals (frost arrows by hand, homing or split ley bolts, a second pylon, the Husk Hive, the Bulwark) inside the new order.
- Whether the build lock covers a saved game already past these steps (proposal: no, a save past step 12 builds freely).

**D. Spec: world and arena polish**
1. Paths: where two paths cross or join, no edge lines inside the join; they read as one shape (an X, not two popsicle sticks).
2. Terrain collision matches the sprite: a small Hollows rock blocks only about its drawn size, not a full tile.
3. The Bulwark's arena walls read as walls: darker than the Hollows floor (only the arena's wall tiles).
4. Arena enemies wait inside: the Warden's (and the Bulwark's) Wisps and Husks don't come to the door or react until the player is inside; no crowd at the doorway.
5. The shrine door's description goes (it's obvious what it does); which text exactly is for the spec (the sealed hint, the open door's hover line, or both).
6. The boss room's hum (the seal's loop while a fight is locked in) a little softer.
7. The rimestone pylon starts 1.5× to 2× its rate today (15 a minute), so frost arrows keep up through the Bulwark.

**Not changing**
- Item name labels: below the item, around it when the player stands close. "That's probably fine."
