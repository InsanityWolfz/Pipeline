# Content brainstorm: what comes after the prototype loop

Oct 19, 2026 · Claude with the playtest agent, at Shaun's request. No code was written.

## How this was made

1. **Playtest.** The playtest agent played the 021–027 build (commit ba9efae) from a fresh start. It went through the tutorial and the shrine claim, compared volley types in a sandbox, then wandered the map. Its screenshots and logs are in the session scratchpad.
2. **Inventory.** A second agent listed everything the game contains today: items, recipes, enemies, armor, the world and the HUD.
3. **Draft and critique.** Claude drafted a direction for each of Shaun's areas. The playtester critiqued it as a player and ranked the ideas. This report merges both.

Every idea is checked against the pillars in docs/design.md:
- offense comes from the factory, defense from the field
- each half has its own reward
- the layout is the ability
- emergence from rules, not content
- no nagging
- the tube is the only weapon

## What the playtest found (the problems to solve)

| Finding | Evidence |
| --- | --- |
| **Nothing heals.** HP only comes back by dying. | Finished the shrine on 10/100 HP and was still on 10 after going home. |
| **Progression stops at the shrine.** | The objective list goes blank. There's no XP, no second site and no boss gate. Packs respawn with nothing new to earn. |
| **The foundry's decisions are shallow.** | The only choice is which of 3 ammo goes into each of 3 entrances. Triple arrows win: 10 s to clear a pack, against 23 s for bolts, 32 s for bombs and 45 s mixed. Bombs land behind moving Husks. A mixed volley fires at the slowest ammo's rate. |
| **Automation stalls.** | The claimed shrine's supply port feeds nothing without a sorter chain the tutorial never teaches. Hoppers can't be fed by conduits. After the tutorial the arrow line was dry. |
| **Ammo runs out fast.** | A 30-round entrance empties in about 7 s of triple fire. Deaths came from running dry, not from misreading attacks. |
| **Drops feel like loose change.** | No rare drop and no "pop" moment. Armor's 2–8 points were never noticeable. |
| **The world is thin.** | 14 nodes are scattered across the meadow, so gathering is a walk. The west and south-east paths end in nothing. The outer ring is forest with packs and no landmarks. |
| **Clutter.** | The materials list reaches 13 rows (about 60% of the screen height) with no names. The HP bar is a thin strip top-left with no number. "+1" popups stack about 7 high, and machine popups overlap. |
| **Lost angle control.** | Removing the stager (024) means the design's "twin cores hit at once" boss has no answer yet. |

## Recommendations, area by area

### 1. Inventory panel, HP bar and bomb casing: do it (quick win)

**Inventory panel**
- An inventory panel on I or Tab, grouped as Raw, Parts, Ammo, Gear and Cores, with names.
- It marks **at risk** (carried since your last deposit) against **banked**. This makes the death rule readable, which is the clever part.
- The HUD drops the material list and keeps a small pickup feed. "+1" popups merge into one counter, and machine popups stop overlapping.

**HP bar**
- Bottom-left, about 3× larger, reading "72 / 100".
- It pulses when low. The armor value sits beside it.

**Bomb casing**
- The bomb casing icon becomes a plain black sphere: the bomb without its fuse or orange band.

### 2. Healing: needed (not on Shaun's list, but the playtest's biggest problem)

- **The playtester's position, which Claude now shares:** no regen in the open field. Heal fast inside a claimed pylon's safe zone and at Ley Springs (a landmark), and heal fully at the foundry.
- **Why:** this keeps the design's tension in "the stretch between your last pylon and wherever you're pushing". Free regen would make the trip home with a schematic safe.
- **Where field regen comes from:** gear affixes and talents ("health regen" is already one of the simple stats), so it's earned, not free.

### 3. Leveling: keep it, but thin

- **XP sources:** kills (scaled by danger ring), bosses, claiming a pylon, and finding a landmark for the first time.
- **No XP from the factory.** Pillar 5 says the factory can't farm combat rewards.
- **Each level gives one talent point**, maybe plus a little max HP. The level is just the pacing clock for the tree.
- **Not fluff:** without it the tree has no fuel. But it shouldn't carry power on its own.

### 4. Skill tree: keep it, start small

- **Size:** 15–20 nodes with 3 keystones for the first version. The playtester judged 40–60 too many to test the feel.
- **Lanes follow the design:**
  - Arrow (physical: crit, pierce)
  - Bomb (fire: radius, burn spreads)
  - Ley (pierce, then lightning chain)
  - Survival (HP, regen, roll)
  - Tube (fire rate, volley)
- **Keystones reward mixing ammo, not more of the same,** since all-one-ammo already wins. Examples:
  - Bomb bursts ignite arrows flying through them.
  - Ley bolts chain to anything burning.
- Combos between entrances are the most fun thing this system can produce.
- **Cut: an active-skill bar.** It breaks "the tube is your only weapon", and the hotbar is already the ammo lines. One keystone alt-fire (overcharge: 3 rounds become one big shot) can be tried later.

### 5. Equipment and affixes: yes, defensive only

- **Slots:** 4 (helm, body, boots, trinket).
- **Rarity:** 1, 2 or 3 affixes, plus rare **relics** that each change one rule.
- **Affixes are defense and utility only:** max HP, field regen, armor, move speed, roll cooldown, slow immunity, ley range (faster refill far from pylons).
- **No damage affixes,** because of pillar 6 (offense from the factory, defense from the field).
- **Keep heavy against light.** The speed trade is the interesting part. Armor values need to be big enough to feel.
- **Cut** "keep X% of your haul on death", which undoes the death rule, and "pickup radius", which 021 already solved.

### 6. New areas and bosses: yes, one region first

- **Shape:** the open map grows outward. Each new region is a wedge at the edge behind a ley barrier that the previous boss's core opens. This fits "boss gates" and "difficulty by distance".
- **Region 2: "Stonefall Hollows"**, at the end of the west path.
  - **Material:** ley-iron.
  - **New enemies:** 2–3 with new behaviours, for example a charger and a burrower, so dodging matters. Ring 2 is still only Husks and Wisps.
  - **Boss: the Bulwark.** It has an armored front, so the answer is arcing shots, which finally gives bombs a niche. It must be slow or slow to turn, or lobs need to lead the target, because bombs miss moving targets.
- **Later regions:** a marsh with a swarm boss (answered by a wide, cheap spread), then a storm spire with a twin-core boss (answered by the Splayer, see 7).

### 7. New things to craft, and better versions of old ones: yes, the heart of factory depth

- **Stage machines** take finished ammo and add a **tag**. Tags are data, so combinations emerge without new code:
  - **Hardener:** pierce.
  - **Ley Guide:** homing.
  - **Runeforge:** adds an element, with the design's behaviours. Cold slows then freezes, lightning chains, poison stacks, fire burns and spreads.
  - **Splayer:** sits between a line and the intake and widens that entrance's fan. This is how angle control comes back, as layout rather than a dial.
- **Example:** a "hardened frost arrow" is just an arrow that passed a Hardener and a frost Runeforge.
- **Regional materials** give alternative, better recipes. For example, a ley-iron arrowhead hits harder.
- **Fixes that come first,** or every later layer has nothing feeding it:
  - Hoppers accept items from conduits.
  - Each entrance fires at its own rate, instead of the whole volley waiting on the slowest.
  - The supply port is taught and actually feeds lines.
- **Deferred as fluff for now:**
  - the Refiner's quality tiers (just +x% numbers)
  - knives as a 4th ammo (wait until tags exist)

### 8. Foundry research tree: yes, presented as restoring the foundry

- **The risk:** two "trees" (character and research) blur together.
- **The answer:** research is restoring the ruined foundry. You activate lost schematics at the intake and at the machines themselves, rather than on a second constellation screen.
- **Costs:** boss cores, machine parts and schematics, which only drop in the field. Never timers.
- **Shaun's first node** is an extra tube entrance after beating the Warden. Proposal: each boss core opens one more entrance:
  - Warden: 2nd entrance
  - Bulwark: 3rd
  - later bosses: 4th and 5th, matching the design's five-arrow pitch
  - Today the Warden core opens both side entrances at once (024), so this would change it.
- **Other early nodes:**
  - reservoir size and refill rate, early, because the playtest showed ammo running dry
  - each stage machine
  - machine speed
  - splitters and mergers
  - blueprints
- **Rare schematic drops** unlock specific nodes. That's the missing "pop" loot moment.

### 9. Landmarks and champions: yes, cheap and high value

- **Placement:** landmarks are data, placed per danger ring and revealed on the minimap when found.
- **Resource sites:** Ley Grotto, Quarry, Old Grove and Reed Marsh, each a dense cluster of nodes. They remove the gathering walk and fill the empty paths. They could later be claimable as supply.
- **Ley Springs:** heal (see 2).
- **Ruined Cache:** a chance at a schematic.
- **Champions:** any non-boss enemy can spawn empowered:
  - about 1.5× size
  - +50% HP, +30% damage, +15% speed
  - 2–3× drops and guaranteed armor
  - a glow and a name
- **Cut: Husk Den.** A champion pack does the same job.

### 10. Something to do after the shrine: needed and cheap

The objective list should never go blank. After the shrine, point at Region 2's barrier, then the Bulwark.

## Suggested order (most fun per cost)

The playtester's ranking, which Claude agrees with:

1. **Foundry flow fixes:** hoppers fed by conduits, each entrance at its own rate, supply port onboarding, and a goal after the shrine.
2. **Stage machines with ammo tags,** plus the Splayer and the element behaviours.
3. **Healing:** safe zones, springs and the foundry.
4. **HUD pass:** HP bar, inventory panel with at-risk marking, black-sphere casing, tidier popups.
5. **Champions and resource landmarks.**
6. **Region 2 and the Bulwark,** plus foundry research (a core per entrance, reservoir upgrades, schematic drops).
7. **New enemy behaviours** for each region.
8. **Equipment slots** with defensive affixes.
9. **Leveling and a 15–20 node tree** with combo keystones.
10. **Later:** overcharge alt-fire, Refiner, knives.
