# Shaun's drive rant, to process after 038

Oct 19, 2026 · Shaun, by voice in chat, while 038 was being built. Saved word for word below. Nothing here is approved design yet. After 038, it becomes a spec interview.

## Themes to process (Claude's index, not decisions)

1. **The hook is a Terraria-style progression.** Recipes show familiar materials beside unknown ones, so the player wants things they can't make yet, and a boss kill connects it all.
2. **The player adapts their factory to each fight.** Bosses and enemies push them to change their layout.
3. **The map grows outward.** New regions sit east and west, or in rings around home, each with special landmarks.
4. **Boss spawners.** The boss waits at a spot in its region. After the first kill, a spawner crafted from that region's resources and drops brings it back, so it can't be spammed. (034's altar offering is close to this.) Maybe bosses drop gear sets, such as a bomb set from a bomb boss: a "big maybe".
5. **Claude should drive content ideas.** Shaun knows the shape he wants but not the specifics. Claude brainstorms, and Shaun checks scope and confusion.
6. **Ammo roles:**
   - Bombs: AOE, many sub-bombs (cluster, elemental), a decent amount of damage, the biggest radius.
   - Ley bolts: homing and splitting, for moving a lot or for walls and dense packs.
   - Arrows: skill shots, more damage than bolts or bombs, for enemies coming straight at you.
   - Landmines: the most damage, on a delay; high risk, high reward.
   - Theme: ancient technology. Enemies as constructs and golems is good theming.
7. **The factory should feel like Factorio but simpler.** Splitters yes; mergers maybe not needed. Later, shared processing: for example a big smelter that is more efficient than single furnaces, with its output split to several setups, so processing throughput becomes a puzzle. It must not get so complex that it turns off combat players.
8. **Foundry upgrades: a bigger foundry,** with room to keep several setups and switch which one feeds the tube for a given fight or area.
9. **Difficulty.** Combat should get harder: more health, more dangerous enemies further out. The Bulwark is too easy, so give it much more health and make it attack faster at low health, for tension.
10. **Make it shippable for friends to playtest:**
    - a title screen with art, the name, New game and Continue
    - a soft save: if the game closes, you respawn in the foundry and lose what you were carrying
    - a start-to-finish flow (state machine) so it feels like a complete game, not a prototype
11. **Plan after this sprint.** Friends playtest the vertical loop, then go wide with horizontal content: new biomes, bosses, projectile types, ways to combine and augment projectiles, augments that affect certain enemies, balance, set bonuses. The vertical loop is: go out, get pylons and new resources, harder enemies, boss materials, foundry upgrades, more complex processing, better ammo, levels and talent points, a full build.
