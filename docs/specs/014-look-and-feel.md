## Goal
The prototype should look and feel like a real game. The rooms get hand-shaped layouts, varied tiles, props and ley light. The key sprites get more detail and animation frames. Every action gets juice (shake, hit-stop, particles, flashes, numbers) and sound. No game rules change.

## Acceptance criteria

**Rooms (data, render)**
- [ ] Each room (clearing, ruin hall, foundry, shrine) gets a non-rectangular, hand-shaped floor. Wall edges are broken up by alcoves, pillars and collapsed sections, all as room data, so collision follows.
- [ ] At least 4 floor tile variants per room theme (cracked flagstone, moss, rune inlay, roots or sand), picked per tile by a fixed hash so they never shimmer. There are wall top and face tiles with corner pieces, so walls read as 3/4 view rather than flat blocks.
- [ ] Decorative props as data, with no collision unless marked: broken columns, rune stones, vines, grass tufts, ley cracks that glow, banners, braziers in the foundry.
- [ ] Ley light: soft additive cyan glows around crystals, ley veins, conduits, pylons and the tube, using render-side light sprites. A gentle dusk tint and vignette per room.
- [ ] Ambient motes: slow-drifting ley particles in every room, and fireflies in the clearing.

**Sprites (art)**
- [ ] Redraws at higher detail within docs/art.md:
  - The player: idle breathe of 4 frames, walk of 6 frames, roll with a smear, fire recoil, hit, death.
  - The Husk, Wisp and Warden: more frames for each telegraph.
  - All nine machines plus the logistics pieces, with working loops that read: spinning parts, glowing runes.
  - Item icons.
  - The HUD frame, rune-carved.
- [ ] A proper title card for the pause screen.

**Juice (render)**
- [ ] Screen shake (small for shots, medium for bomb bursts and Warden slams, big for KO) and hit-stop (2–4 frames on enemy hits and kills, render-only, so the sim never pauses). Shake can be turned off by the reduced-motion preference.
- [ ] Particles: muzzle flash per shot; hit sparks in each damage type's colour; death dissolve into motes; footstep dust; roll dust trail; pickup sparkle; harvest chips; burst embers; machine puffs on each craft; refill shimmer at the socket.
- [ ] Damage numbers that pop and float, white on enemies and red on the player.
- [ ] Squash and stretch on pickups, hopper deposits and placed pieces (a small drop-in bounce). Hover and press feedback on all menu rows.
- [ ] A short camera lead toward the aim direction.

**Sound (data, render)**
- [ ] Sounds are generated by a new `npm run sound` build script. It's a small synth in Node with no new dependency, and writes WAV files to `assets/build/sounds`, like the art pipeline. A check keeps them in step.
- [ ] Effects:
  - fire, one per ammo type
  - hit
  - enemy hurt and death
  - Husk wind-up, Wisp charge, Warden slam
  - player hurt and KO
  - pickup, harvest tick, craft
  - place, pick-up, rotate
  - machine craft
  - refill, attune swell
  - pylon claim, travel whoosh
  - menu click and hover
  - dry fire
- [ ] Ambient loops per room (wind and birds in the clearing, a low hum in the foundry, a chime drone in the shrine) crossfade on room change.
- [ ] Master volume and mute (M) are saved in localStorage, with a guarded read. Audio starts on the first input, per browser rules.
- [ ] Sound keys come from `src/data/sounds.ts`, never string literals.

**Rules unchanged**
- [ ] No sim rule changes. Room collision changes are data. Every existing unit and e2e test passes, updated only for new room tiles, spawn points and positions.

**Checks**
- [ ] `npm run check` and `npm run test:e2e` pass. All new art and sound are pending Shaun's review, with a contact sheet of before and after at 4× in the report.
