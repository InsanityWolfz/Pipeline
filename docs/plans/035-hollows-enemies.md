# Plan 035: Region 2 enemies

Spec: docs/specs/035-hollows-enemies.md, with Shaun's decisions in docs/brainstorm/2026-10-19-open-questions.md (H: the Shardback reuses 034's shield rule, straight shots and punches into the front blocked, lobs and mines always land; 035.1–035.9 accepted as proposed). Written for the unattended run (028–038 as separate commits on `claude/laughing-volta-uac3nf`).

## Shape of the change
Small vertical commits, one enemy at a time, failing tests first in each. Each enemy's first commit is its data (with its rig, anims and wind-up sound, which the data tests require of every kind), its AI and its sim tests; its second is render (telegraph, overlays, poses, sounds played). Then packs, drops and population; then the e2e and review shots.

1. **Shared (data, sim).**
   - `src/data/enemies.ts`: `MIN_TELEGRAPH_TICKS` (30); `EnemyBase`, `AttackTiming` and `ScreenLeashed` exported; the union gains the four kinds, whose interfaces and entries live in the new `src/data/hollowsEnemies.ts` (as `bulwark.ts` does for the Bulwark); `attackOf` covers them. A data test: every enemy's wind-up (and the Bulwark's lob) is at least `MIN_TELEGRAPH_TICKS`.
   - `src/sim/sightLine.ts` (new): an exact tile walk (DDA) along a segment, `clearGround` (no tile solid to feet) and `clearShot` (no tile that stops shots), and `shotReach` (how far a straight line runs before a tile stops it). The punch's wall test moves onto it.
   - `EnemyState` gains `underground` (Burrower), `spotX/spotY` (the Charger's lane start, the Burrower's locked spot), `struck` (the Charger's hit this charge) and `bumped` (its last move hit a wall); `EnemyMode` gains `stun`, `dig` and `peek`.
   - `targetable(e)`: false underground. Arrows (straight, piercing, homing locks), punches and bombs skip an untargetable enemy. `separate` skips an enemy underground or charging.
   - Champions scale `charge.speed` and `burrow.speed` with walking (+15%) and every attack's damage; timings, ranges, lanes, rings, arcs and the shield stay the enemy's own.
   - Events `charges`, `eruptions`, `snipes`. `getState().enemies[]` gains `angle` (degrees, shielded only), `lane`, `underground`, `locked` and `snipe` (phase and line) where the kind has them.
2. **Charger** (`src/sim/chargerAi.ts`): aggro on screen; walks at the player; within `triggerRange` with a clear ground line and its cooldown ready it plants and winds up 45 ticks with the lane locked from its feet; charges 360 px/s down the lane, striking once per charge a player whose feet box touches its body (a circle of half the lane's width round its feet) for 25 and 48 px knockback along the lane (i-frames dodge); stops at the lane's end and holds still 45; a wall first stuns it 60 instead (no damage to it); passes through enemies.
3. **Burrower** (`src/sim/burrowerAi.ts`): idles above ground; on aggro digs in for 20 ticks (hittable); underground tunnels at 70 px/s with normal collision, untargetable, not separated; within 12 px of the feet or after 180 ticks (cooldown ready) locks the player's feet, its ripple moving to the spot; winds up 50 (the ring); erupts for 22 and 32 px outward; stays surfaced and dazed 150 (hittable), then digs in again.
4. **Shardback** (`src/sim/shardbackAi.ts`): 034's shield (180°, 120°/s); walks at the player turning its shield; within 30 px winds up 40 ticks still turning; bashes the 100° arc, 40 px reach, along its facing, for 20 and 40 px; recovers 30 without turning.
5. **Sniper construct** (`src/sim/sniperAi.ts`, `src/sim/cover.ts`): aggros on screen or within 320 px; cover is a free tile within 5 tiles with a shot-stopping tile on its line to the player's feet, found from the grid each tick; walks to the nearest and waits while its cooldown runs, or keeps its distance in the open like the Wisp; cooldown ready, it steps to the nearest tile within 1 with a clear line (or shoots from where it stands) and winds up: 60 tracking (a blocked line cancels and it re-peeks), 24 locked; fires one bolt in the orb list (480 px/s, 28, 400 px, ends at solids, a roll dodges it) and goes back to cover; within 96 px it retreats to the nearest cover farther from the player.
6. **Drops, packs, population.** Every one drops 1 ley-iron, a 50% stone, a 4% aqueduct valve (`REGION_2_PART`, 12% for a champion), the shared schematic roll and 10% armor. Ring 3: 6 packs of 4–5 from `['shardback', 'charger', 'charger', 'burrower', 'sniper']` with `limits` (1 sniper, 2 burrowers): a member is picked from the pool still under its limit. The Cistern Terrace's guards: 2 Shardbacks, 2 Chargers, 1 Burrower, 1 Sniper. Ring 3 champions 1 in 4, names for the four.
7. **Hook:** `spawnEnemy(enemyId, x, y, champion?)` in the dev hook, to put a test pack of each kind in the `?start=hollows` sandbox.
8. **Render, art, sound:**
   - Rigs in `src/data/art` (charger, burrower, shardback, sniper) in grey-blue stone, brass, rust and cyan, with idle, walk, wind-up, attack, recover, hit, death, and the Charger's `stun`, the Burrower's `dig` (and its ripple sprite), the Shardback's `block` and `turn`, the Sniper's `step`. `ENEMY_ANIMS` keys; a wind-up can hold several frames (the Charger pawing).
   - Telegraphs in the shared enemy red (`ENEMY_FX.telegraphColor`, as the Warden's): the lane (faint, filling with the wind-up), the cracked ring growing at the locked spot over the still ripple, the bash arc following the shield, the laser from the lens (thin while tracking, bright and blinking once locked). EnemyView's telegraphs move to their own file to stay under 300 lines.
   - The ripple (moving mound with a dust trail), the horns' glow, the runes' flare and the lens glow; the bolt in flight.
   - Sounds: charge wind-up and impact, rumble and erupt, bash (the clang is 034's), laser hum and lock beep, the bolt.
9. **e2e and shots:** `tests/e2e/hollowsEnemies.spec.ts` (each fight) and `tests/e2e/hollowsEnemiesShots.spec.ts` (REVIEW_SHOTS=1 into docs/review/035).

## Not dictated by the spec
- Body sizes (feet boxes and hurtboxes), hit knockback, knock ticks (Charger 8, Burrower 6, Shardback 6, Sniper 4) and attack-pose lengths.
- The Charger's body for its hit is a circle of half the lane's width round its feet, so the lane drawn is exactly what it sweeps. Within trigger range and clear line, it stands and waits out its cooldown rather than walking onto the player. A wall's stun replaces the recovery ("recover 45 (stun 60 on a wall)").
- The Burrower's ripple moves to the locked spot when it locks (12 px or less in the common case; after the 180-tick timeout it jumps to under the player, sliding against walls), so the ripple, the ring and the eruption share one point. Underground it walks home at its walk speed and surfaces there.
- The Shardback walks straight at the player while its shield turns on its own (as the Bulwark), and bashes along its shield's facing at the bash.
- The Sniper's open-ground distance is 200 px (the Wisp's settle of 2 px); its bolt's box is 6 px; a wind-up already running isn't dropped when the player closes in (only a new one isn't started); with no tile within a step that sees the player it walks toward them until one does; cover tiles are walkable and outside any safe zone; the line is feet to feet (the bolt's ground line).
- The stone chance is the Husk's 50% for all four; the valve's champion chance is 12% (3×, as Region 1's part).
- Champion names for the four are placeholders.

## Build notes
- **Every telegraph minimum holds, old and new** (`tests/data/hollowsEnemies.test.ts`): the Husk 36, the Wisp 48, the Warden 60, the Bulwark's pound 60 and lob 30, the Charger 45, the Burrower 50, the Shardback 40, the Sniper 84 (60 + 24).
- **Sight lines** walk the exact tiles a segment crosses (`src/sim/sightLine.ts`), so cover and the Sniper's line cost a handful of tile lookups each; the punch's wall test now uses the same walk (its old 4 px sampling gave the same answers in every test).
- **The Charger** checks its strike, then a wall it ran into last tick (`bumped`, set by the move), then the lane's end. Its run is counted along the lane, so a safe zone's edge (keepOut) stops it short and it recovers at the lane's end without moving.
- **The Burrower** is a `dig` mode, then `chase` with `underground` set (its `modeTicks` count the ticks under); `targetable` keeps it out of arrows (straight, piercing, homing locks), punches and bomb bursts, and `passesThrough` out of separation (a charging Charger too). It never locks a player standing in a safe zone; it waits at the edge.
- **The Shardback** uses 034's `shield.ts` unchanged; EnemyView picks a shielded enemy's pose (turn, block) by `def.shield`, so it shares the Bulwark's.
- **The Sniper's bolt** is an orb whose numbers come from its shooter's kind (`orbs.ts`), so a roll's i-frames, walls and the barrier treat it as the Wisp's; `getState().orbs` now names each shooter. Tracking runs the wind-up's first 60 ticks (its start among them), locked the last 24; the laser blinks on the sim's ticks, so a held sim holds the blink (the shots and e2e read it from `ui().hollowsEnemies`).
- **Ring 3's champion chance draws once per reset** for the Cistern Terrace's guards. That moved where `?playtest`'s ring 2 packs fall, and 017's crowd e2e walked into a tree: it now picks open ground half a screen from the busiest pack. No seeded sim test moved.
- `EnemyView.update` takes the sim (the laser runs to the player); its telegraphs moved to `telegraphs.ts` and Region 2's extras to `hollowsTelegraphs.ts`, `hollowsEnemyArt.ts` and `hollowsEnemyFeel.ts`. `BulwarkDef` moved beside its values in `bulwark.ts`, keeping `enemies.ts` under 300 lines.
- The dev hook's `spawnEnemy(enemyId, x, y, champion?)` stages the sandbox's packs; e2e and shots share `tests/e2e/hollowsSandbox.ts`.
