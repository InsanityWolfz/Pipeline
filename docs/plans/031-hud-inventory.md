# Plan 031: HUD pass: inventory panel and HP bar

Spec: docs/specs/031-hud-inventory.md (approved, open questions settled in docs/brainstorm/2026-10-19-open-questions.md, 031.1–031.7 and L). Written for the unattended run (028–038 as separate commits on `claude/laughing-volta-uac3nf`). 030 (healing) is built, so the cyan glow criterion is in. The spec asks for no docs/prototype.md or docs/design.md change.

## Shape of the change
Small vertical commits, tests first in each:

1. **At-risk helper (sim).** `src/sim/banking.ts` gains `itemRisks(state)`: per carried item (count > 0) `{ held, safe, atRisk }` with `safe = min(held, banked)`, and `atRiskCounts(state)`: only the items with at least one at risk. `wake()` in `src/sim/health.ts` keeps `safe` and loses `atRisk` through that helper, so the two can't disagree (the rule itself is unchanged). `getState().atRisk`.
2. **Data.**
   - `src/data/items.ts`: `ItemGroup = 'raw' | 'parts' | 'ammo' | 'gear' | 'cores'` and a `group` on every item (tagged rounds: `ammo`; the warden core: `cores`, its `kind` stays `part`).
   - `src/data/hudPanels.ts` (new, beside `hudLayout.ts`, which is near its size limit): `HUD_HEALTH_LAYOUT` (bottom-left, `HUD_LAYOUT.margin` in, a 152×20 carved frame round a 144×12 fill, fill colours `leaf` / `hit-red`, the chunk's `snow`, the shield's layout and dim alpha) replacing the old one in `assets.ts`, and `HP_BAR` (`lowFraction` 0.25, `pulseMs` 900, pulse scale and brighten, the edge tint's colour/alpha/inner edge, `chunkHoldMs` 400, `chunkDrainMs` 300, `riseMs` for the smooth rise, the glow's colour, blur and fade). `HUD_ARMOR_LAYOUT` goes.
   - Also in `src/data/hudPanels.ts`: `INVENTORY_PANEL` (group order and labels, row and header sizes, column x offsets, pads, the title, the "Nothing" text, the footer text, the colours) with the panel's size and its column split computed from `ITEMS` (each group reserves a row per item of the group, Gear two more for the worn and spare lines, so the layout never jumps), and `PICKUP_FEED` (`holdMs` 2500, `fadeMs` 400, `maxLines` 5, top-left position and line step).
   - `src/data/assets.ts`: `HUD_MATERIALS_LAYOUT`, the old `HUD_HEALTH_LAYOUT` and `PICKUP_POPUP` go; `MADE_POPUP` gains `stackGap` 2 and `mergeMs`; new sprite keys (`hudHealthGlow`, `hudShield`, `hudWarn`, `inventoryPanel`, `inventoryTitle`; `hudHealth` keeps its key, redrawn larger); `FX_TEXTURES.lowHp`; `DEPTHS.lowHp` between the mood and the HUD.
3. **Art (data/art).** `src/data/art/hud.ts`: the larger HP frame (carved stone, brass ends, a dark well), its cyan glow ring, the shield icon (a brass-rimmed heater shield with a ley rune), the at-risk warning mark (a rust-light triangle with an ink "!"), the inventory tablet (rune course along its top, like the crafting panel) and its title card. `src/data/art/items.ts`: the casing becomes the bomb's sphere (same radial fill, outline and highlight) with no fuse, spark or cinder band; the bomb icon is built from the same shapes plus the band, wick and spark, so it is unchanged; the header comment is updated.
4. **HP bar (render).** `src/render/healthBarModel.ts` (pure): the shown fill (drops at once; rises smoothly over `riseMs` while healing, else jumps), the damage chunk (a drop keeps the old top white, a hit during the hold grows it and restarts the hold, it drains over `chunkDrainMs` after `chunkHoldMs`, healing past it clears it), `low` (below `lowFraction`, never while dying) and the pulse phase. `src/render/healthLayout.ts` (pure): the HP block's box, at the bottom-left, or raised to sit `gap` above the build bar's label row when building and the bar's left edge comes within `gap` of it. `src/render/HealthBar.ts`: the frame, fill, chunk, glow, "72 / 100" (health rounded up) in the count font centred on the bar, and the shield with the worn armor value (dimmed "0" with nothing worn). `src/render/EdgeTint.ts`: the red screen edges (a white edge gradient tinted `hit-red`, drawn under the HUD), pulsing with the bar while low.
5. **Pickup feed (render).** `src/render/pickupFeedModel.ts` (pure): merge by item (a repeat adds to the line, restarts its timer and moves it to the top), newest on top, at most `maxLines` (the oldest leaves), hold then fade. `src/render/PickupFeed.ts` draws it top-left from the sim's `pickups` events only. `EffectsView` loses the "+1 [icon]" popups; the sparkle and the 021 slide stay.
6. **Inventory panel (render).** `src/render/inventoryRows.ts` (pure): the groups in order with a row per carried item (count, at risk from the helper, home from the stash), Gear's worn and spare lines. `src/render/InventoryPanel.ts`: the tablet and title card centred, two columns when one won't fit (it doesn't: Raw and Parts left; Ammo, Gear and Cores right), group headers, a dim "Nothing" under an empty group, each row's icon, name, count and mark (all safe: a cyan "safe"; any at risk: "N safe ·" in cyan then the warning mark and "N at risk" in `rust-light`), a dim "home N", and the footer. Read-only.
7. **Keys and menus (render).** `keys.ts`: I and Tab (Phaser captures both, so Tab never moves focus). `Controls.takeInventoryToggle()`. `MenuRouter`: I/Tab toggles the panel unless the pause menu or the wake panel is up; Esc closes it first; opening it closes the crafting menu, the hopper picker and a site menu; opening any of those closes it. The panel doesn't pause; `HudView.overUi` covers it, so a click on it never fires; the 021 pointer shows while it's open.
8. **HudView** owns the HP bar, edge tint, pickup feed and inventory panel (so GameScene, at its limit, only swaps one call); the materials column and the top-left armor icon go. GameScene hands it the sim, the frame's events and the frame time.
9. **Machine popups (render).** `src/render/popupStack.ts` (pure): a new popup's box that would overlap a live one starts `stackGap` above that popup's top (repeated until clear); a popup for the same machine and item within `mergeMs` merges into a count ("+ shaft ×2"). `MadePopups` uses it, rises linearly (so stacked popups keep their gap; only the fade eases) and reports each live popup's text and screen box.
10. **Hook.** `ui().inventory { open, groups: [{ id, rows: [{ itemId, count, atRisk, home }] }] }`, `ui().pickupFeed [{ itemId, count }]` newest first, `ui().health { box, text, low, healing, chunk }`, `ui().madePopups [{ text, x, y, w, h }]`.

## Not dictated by the spec
- The helper is `itemRisks` / `atRiskCounts`; `wake()` uses it (no rule change).
- A feed line that repeats moves to the top (it is the newest), so the oldest by last pickup is the one that leaves.
- The panel reserves room for every item of each group, so it is one fixed size (a vector tablet) and rows never jump; groups never split across columns.
- Gear's worn and spare lines report as rows with `itemId` `worn` and `spare` in `ui()`; the spare line shows only with at least one spare, the worn line only with a piece worn.
- The HP block's `box` in `ui()` is the frame through the armor value.
- The HP bar's chunk, rise, pulse and glow, and the feed's hold and fade, run on tween time (the frame's delta times the tween speed), so the hook's `tweenSpeed` slows or holds them like every other effect; the chunk test lowers health with `setPlayerHealth` rather than a fight. Game time runs behind the wall clock when headless frames are slow, so waits on these use longer poll timeouts.
- The pulse scales the frame, fill and chunk about the bar's centre and washes the fill toward white; the text and shield stay still.
- The edge tint is its own white edge-gradient texture tinted `hit-red`, at a depth between the room mood and the HUD.
- Popups rise linearly (their fade still eases), so stacked popups keep their gap as they rise.
- Merged popups read "+ shaft ×2".
- One spare reads "1 spare piece (kept)"; more read "N spare pieces (kept)".
- The worn line reads "worn · heavy · armor 5" in the safe colour, with the weight's armor icon; the spares line uses the shield icon.
- The e2e that needs the foundry on screen (the popups) waits until no meadow nodes are drawn, since the door's fade lags the sim's room.
- `HUD_HEALTH_LAYOUT`, `HP_BAR`, `INVENTORY_PANEL` and `PICKUP_FEED` live in a new `src/data/hudPanels.ts` (not `hudLayout.ts`, which would pass 300 lines).

## Risks
- `HudView.update` changes signature; GameScene must stay under ~300 lines.
- The two-popups e2e depends on the cutter's and knapper's steps finishing in the same batch of ticks; it steps them in one call so both pops land in one draw.
