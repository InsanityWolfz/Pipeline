# Plan 040: Legibility UI

Spec: docs/specs/040-legibility.md (approved on draft, 2026-10-19; its open questions 040.1–040.7 each carry a proposal that this build follows). Built on `claude/laughing-volta-uac3nf` after 039, reusing 039's data and helpers: `KEYWORDS`, `NOUNS`, content `lines`, `STATS`, `rulesText.ts` (the one formatter, `LINE_SEP`, `stageLineText`), `roundKeywords.ts`, `keywordGraph.ts` (sources and payoffs), `keywordReport.ts`, `resolveStat` and `emissions.ts`. No rule changes; no new dependency; docs/design.md, backlog and progress untouched.

## Shape of the change
Small vertical commits, tests first in each.

1. **Data and the shared tablet pieces.**
   - `src/data/legibility.ts` (new): `TIP` (summary 200 px and breakdown 280 px wide, 16 lines of 10 px, pad, gap, the 4 px edge margin, `graceMs` 200, colours), `CHIP` (padding, height, radius, a palette id per group: delivery, behaviour, damage type, status, ground, family, trait, plus All ammo and You), `BUILD_TAB` (14 px rows, columns, header style), `GLOSSARY` (260 × 170, the section labels "Sources", "Payoffs", "Changes", "None yet"), `TALENT_TABS`, the words ("dmg", "N bonuses · hold Shift", "Reaches:", "Reaches: nothing you have yet", "Linked:", "Takes:", the breakdown's "when" templates for Pool and Freeze, the scope entries for All ammo and You), and the map from each keyword param to the stat and emission that resolve it (so "yours:" and final numbers come from `resolveStat`).
   - `src/data/fonts.ts`: a `chip` text style (body face). `src/data/rulesText.ts` gains `precise` time formatting (2 decimals where 1 isn't exact, 039.8) and `formatDamage` (1 decimal, no trailing ".0"); both feed the one formatter.
   - `src/render/KeywordChip.ts`: a rounded chip in its group's colour, screen or world. `src/render/RuleTip.ts`: the stone tablet (020) with the name in the display face, chips flowing after it (a second row only if they don't fit), body lines word-wrapped to the width, coloured segments (Linked's lit names), at most 16 rows, chip hit boxes. `src/render/tipPlace.ts` (pure): beside an anchor in order of preference, flipping side or moving to stay 4 px inside 640×360, or docked in a box's corner (037's info area).
2. **Describe module** (`src/render/describe.ts` and helpers, no Phaser): `{ name, chips, summary, breakdown, bonuses }` for a round, a stage, a talent, a gear piece (relics included), a build-bar piece and a keyword, from 039's data and the live state (`resolveStat`, `playerStats`, `keywordSources`, `keywordPayoffs`). Rounds: chips family, delivery, behaviours, damage type (040.3); summary "11.6 dmg · 4.0/s · Freeze in 4 hits" etc.; breakdown one line per stat ("Damage 10 × (1 + 10% Fletcher's Eye + 6% Hard Bore) = 11.6", "Fire rate … · one every 0.21 s", Overpressure as "× up to 1.25 while held 2.0 s"), "when" lines (Pool, Shatterpoint), each keyword's rule with its final numbers (px included), and "Linked:". Talents and relics: generated lines and "Reaches:"; breakdown before → after on every emission each line reaches (Wide Casing's four Burst radii), lane and cost. Stages, gear and the glossary as the spec lists.
3. **Hover summaries per kind**: a `Legibility` view (one aggregator in GameScene) asks the HUD (hotbar, tube slot, inventory rows, talent nodes, Build tab rows), the site menus (bench Stages rows, gear rows and worn cells) and the build bar for the thing under the cursor; it keeps the tablet while the cursor is on the source or the tablet, with `TIP.graceMs` on leaving (040.1). The build-bar tip (015, `BuildTip.ts` and `BUILD_TIP`) and the talent info strip (037) go; `ui().buildTip` and `ui().talents.hover` keep reporting what they did.
4. **Shift + hover**: Shift (a new key binding, read as held) expands the tablet in place to the breakdown; release returns the summary. Shift+click in hoppers keeps 022's meaning (it reads the click's own event).
5. **Build tab**: the T panel's title card gets Talents and Build tabs in 036's tab style; the panel remembers its tab while the game runs. The Build tab (`src/render/BuildTab.ts`, layout pure in `buildTabLayout.ts`) lists every known round under a header per family (a family with none hidden), each row its icon, name, shape and element chips, final damage, shots/s and "N sources"; two columns only when the rows don't fit (031's rule). Hovering a row shows its breakdown beside the panel without Shift; in the Build tab the panel shifts left so the 280 px breakdown sits beside it inside the view.
   - **Known rounds (040.2)**: a sim list `carried` (ammo items ever in the inventory, kept through a KO) in `src/sim/knownRounds.ts`; `knownRounds(state)` = hand-crafted or hand-applied (`crafted`), made by a machine (`machineMade`), or carried.
6. **Chips and the glossary**: clicking a chip on a tip, a Build tab row or the glossary opens that keyword's entry (`src/render/GlossaryView.ts`): centred above any panel, at most 260 × 170, name, group and chip; the rule with the data's numbers and "yours:" beside numbers the player's bonuses change; Sources (from `keywordSources`: known rounds, stages, relics, keystones, keywords), Payoffs (from `keywordPayoffs`, "None yet" for none and after a debt keyword's), Changes (its stats); the keywords its rule names as chips, which replace the entry. Esc or a click outside closes it (that click never fires); it never pauses. All ammo and You have their own entries (039.1); reserved keywords have none.
7. **Enemy hover (040.4)**: the hover name gains trait chips (Boss, Shield, Burrowed) and live status chips ("Chill 2", "Freeze", "Stun"), not clickable.
8. **Hook, e2e, shots**: `ui()` gains `tip`, `talentPanel`, `buildTab`, `glossary` and `hoverChips`; the descriptions.ts font comment is corrected.

## Not dictated by the spec
- "At most four rows": the name row (chips wrapping to a second row only if they don't fit), up to two rules lines and the hint; a rules line wider than the tablet wraps; a relic's gear summary adds its rule lines.
- The hint's N counts the distinct talents, relics and affixes in play that change a number the tip shows (for content, the numbers its own lines change), so a new player never sees it (040.7). Its singular is "1 bonus".
- Summary and Build tab fire rates are without Overpressure's held ramp; the breakdown shows the ramp as its own "× up to" step.
- "Reaches:" is left off a talent or relic whose lines are all scope You (no round to reach). A status-scoped line (Rimecraft) reaches the rounds whose keywords make that status (Cold → Chill → Freeze).
- A breakdown's before → after lists each emission a line reaches once: shape-tagged rounds only when they differ from their base, children (Shard, Bomblet, Pool-burst) by name.
- "Linked:" lists talents, relics and stages whose lines touch one of the round's keywords (or one its keywords make), or a stage that can tag it; All ammo and You lines touch every round and are left out.
- Glossary Sources list only rounds the player knows (as the Build tab hides unseen rounds), and every stage, relic, keystone and keyword source.
- The talent summary docks in the panel's bottom strip (037's info area), on the side away from the hovered node, growing up.
- While the glossary is open the whole screen is its: a click outside closes it and never fires.

## Build notes
- Commits in order: plan; shared tablet and chips (data, formatter, placement, KeywordChip, RuleTip); the describe module and known rounds; hover summaries and Shift (the Legibility view, the providers, BuildTip and the talent info strip replaced, enemy chips); the Build tab and the glossary; e2e; review shots.
- The describe module is split by kind to stay under 300 lines: `describe.ts` (the dispatcher and enemy chips), `describeRound.ts`, `describeContent.ts` (talents, gear, stages, build-bar pieces), `describeGlossary.ts`, `describeChanges.ts` (before → after), `describeLinks.ts` (Reaches and Linked), `keywordRule.ts` (a keyword's rule with numbers and "yours:"), `statSteps.ts` (a stat's steps and chips). None imports Phaser.
- `TALENT_PANEL` gains `tabs` (legibility.ts names it `TALENT_TABS`); `TALENT_PANEL.info` keeps the info area's top and gains `infoGap`; its title text no longer draws (the tabs take the card).
- In the Build tab the panel moves left (x 28) so a 280 px breakdown sits beside it inside the view; the breakdown follows the hovered row's height.
- The bonus hint counts a content piece's own change (a taken or hovered talent's line, a worn piece's affixes), so a stat talent's summary always ends "1 bonus · hold Shift"; a rule-only talent (a keystone) shows none.
- The glossary's Payoffs reads "Blastfletch (more: None yet)" for a debt keyword with payoffs, "None yet" for one without; a trait has no payoffs and reads "None yet".
- Before → after writes the unit once ("48 → 55.2 px"); a % stays on both sides ("100% → 104%").
- A breakdown longer than 16 rows (a Frost Split ley bolt with most of the tree taken) ends its last row with "…"; Linked sits before the keyword rules so it's never the part cut.
- Shift is bound without capture, so the page still sees it on clicks (022's Shift+click in hoppers).
- The rounds a player knows are a new sim list, `carried` (ammo ever in the inventory), with `crafted` and `machineMade` (src/sim/knownRounds.ts).
