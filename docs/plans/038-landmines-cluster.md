# Plan 038: Landmines and the cluster stage

Spec: docs/specs/038-landmines-cluster.md (approved; text moved verbatim out of 034, with open questions 038.1–038.11 each carrying a proposed answer that this build follows). Written for the unattended run (028–038 as separate commits on `claude/laughing-volta-uac3nf`). The spec asks for no docs/design.md or docs/prototype.md change.

## Shape of the change
Small vertical commits, tests first in each:

1. **Landmines (data, sim).**
   - `src/data/ammo.ts`: a `MineAmmo` kind `'mine'` (speed, maxRange and arcPeak copied from the bomb's, `fuseTicks` 300, `damage` 60, `radius` 24, physical, `fireTicks` 40 — 038.7), `landmine` among the bases; `lobbedAmmo(id)` for anything that arcs (a bomb or a mine).
   - `src/sim/bombs.ts`: a mine flies as a bomb does (the same flight, arc and barrier stop) but lands instead of bursting: it arms (`src/sim/mines.ts`). Its damage is fixed at the throw through `playerStats` (all-ammo damage only, 038.5); its flight isn't shortened by Short Fuse.
   - `src/sim/mines.ts`: `armMine`, `stepMines` (runs before bombs fly, so a mine that lands starts at fuse 0 and bursts `fuseTicks` later; an armed mine burns its fuse and, at `fuseTicks`, bursts through `blastArea`: every live, targetable enemy within its radius, no knockback, never the player, never asking a shield). `mineArms` and `mineBursts` events. Any room change (a door or travel: the tick's `roomChanges`) clears armed mines and mines in flight (038.8); a KO's `resetWorld` clears them too.
   - `src/data/tube.ts`: `SLOT_AMMOS` gains `landmine` (key 4). `volley.ts`: lobbed rounds of any arcing kind land side by side, `LOB_SPACING` apart. `stats.ts`: `ofBase` and the ammo stats cover `landmine` (all-ammo bonuses only).
   - `src/data/items.ts`: `iron-plate` (part, Parts) and `landmine` (ammo, Ammo). `src/data/recipes.ts`: `iron-plate` (1 ley-iron → 2) and `landmine` (1 iron plate + 1 fuse + 1 ley shard → 2), each `shownAfter: 'ley-iron'`; a `landmine` crafting group. `src/data/pieces.ts`: a "Mines" build-bar group with the plate press (1×1, 60 ticks) and the mine packer (3×1, 90 ticks) (038.1).
   - Picked up (038.3): `state.found` lists the reveal items (`shownAfter` values) ever carried, from the start's kit or any tick's inventory, kept through a KO; `knownRecipe` reads it.
2. **The cluster stage (data, sim).**
   - `src/data/tags.ts`: tag `cluster` (shape, bombs, prefix "Cluster", behaviour `cluster`) and `CLUSTER` (`delayTicks` 12, `count` 4, `distance` 40, `damage` 8, `radius` 20). `bomb+cluster` and its item are derived, as 029's rounds are.
   - `src/data/stages.ts`: the Shard die (`shard-die`, 4 bombs + 1 ley-iron → 4 cluster bombs, 60 ticks), so its machine, bench row and recipe come from 029's derivations; `pieces.ts` puts it in the Bombs group.
   - `src/sim/bombs.ts`/`mines.ts`: a cluster bomb bursts as a bomb does, then queues `count` bomblets round the burst point at `distance`, the first along the bomb's flight (038.9), each bursting `delayTicks` later for the bomb's damage share of `CLUSTER.damage` in `playerStats().ammo.bombletRadius` (038.5) through `blastArea`. `bombletBursts` event. Blastfletch charges only the bomb's own burst (038.6).
3. **Hook.** `getState()` gains `mines` (id, room, x, y, fuse, fuseTicks), `bomblets` (id, room, x, y, from, ticks left) and `found`, in a new `summaryMines.ts`; the events `mineArms`, `mineBursts`, `bombletBursts`.
4. **Render.**
   - Art (`src/data/art/mines.ts`, keys in `src/data/mineAssets.ts`): the landmine in flight and its shadow, the armed mine, its burst flash, the landmine and iron-plate icons, the bomblet, the cluster tag's badge (in 029's `TAG_ART`), and the mechanisms of the plate press, the mine packer and the Shard die (`src/data/art/mineMechanisms.ts`). Shaun approves by eye (038.11).
   - `MineView` (owned by `RoomViews`): armed mines with a fuse ring filling and reddening, bomblets arcing out over their delay, mine and bomblet burst flashes; `ui().mines`.
   - Feel (`mineFeel.ts`, from `FeelView`): mine arm and burst sounds (`src/data/mineSounds.ts`, generated by `npm run sound`), the bomblets' quieter burst, juice bursts and shakes (038.10).
   - The crafting menu in two balanced columns (038.4): Arrows and Bombs, then Ley and Landmines.
   - `GameScene.ts` stays under 300 lines: the craft menu's update takes the sim, the mines draw from `RoomViews`.

## Tests (written first)
- **Vitest** `tests/sim/mines.test.ts`: the landmine's numbers; it lands at the aim like a bomb (same flight ticks), arms there and bursts 300 ticks after landing for 60 within 24 px (an enemy at 22 px hit, one at 28 px not); an enemy walking over it doesn't set it off; it never hurts the player; a shield's front doesn't block it; two mines in a volley land `LOB_SPACING` apart; a room change and a KO clear armed mines and mines in flight; no cap (several live at once); key 4 selects it; talents: Hard Bore raises its damage, Black Powder doesn't; determinism.
- **Vitest** `tests/sim/cluster.test.ts`: the tag and its round (`bomb+cluster`, "Cluster bomb", one shape only); the Shard die makes 4 cluster bombs from 4 bombs and 1 ley-iron in 60 ticks (the stage cost spent) and refuses a cluster bomb; hand-applying it at the bench unlocks it; a cluster bomb bursts as a bomb, then 4 bomblets burst 12 ticks later at 40 px, 90° apart, the first along the flight, each for 8 in 20 px; shields don't block them; Blastfletch charges once.
- **Vitest** `tests/sim/mineChain.test.ts`: the recipes and machines (plate press, mine packer) unlock by hand-crafting; the recipes are hidden until ley-iron is carried and stay known after a KO; a plate press and mine packer line makes landmines.
- **Vitest** data: the new items' groups, recipes' references, machine ids in `MACHINE_IDS`, build-bar groups; the crafting menu's two columns fit the view (`tests/render/bottomHud.test.ts`).
- **Rewritten:** tests that assume three hotbar slots, three crafting groups or one crafting column.
- **Playwright** `tests/e2e/mines.spec.ts`: in `?start=hollows`, select key 4 and fire a landmine at a spawned Husk standing still on it, see it armed in `getState().mines` and its burst kill the Husk; hand-apply the Shard die at the bench, fire a cluster bomb and see its bomblets burst.
- **Playwright** `tests/e2e/minesShots.spec.ts` (REVIEW_SHOTS=1, into docs/review/038/): an armed mine with its fuse ring part-filled, a cluster burst with its bomblets (tweens slowed), the crafting menu's two columns, the hotbar with key 4, and the new machines working in a line.

## Not dictated by the spec
See the spec's open questions 038.1–038.11: each proposal is built as written. Also:
- A mine that meets the shut ley barrier mid-flight drops there and arms, as a bomb bursts there.
- A mine's damage scales with all-ammo damage talents at the throw; its radius doesn't scale.
- Bomblets burst only on enemies in the room they were thrown in, as bombs do, and vanish with the player's room change like mines.

## Risks
- `state.ts`, `summary.ts`, `hudLayout.ts`, `sounds.ts` and `GameScene.ts` are near 300 lines: new state goes in one `mines` block, the summary in its own file, sound recipes in their own file.
- The crafting panel grows wider; the 026 e2e checks headers and rows by id, not position.
